# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and engineering teams evaluating Benjamin Takaki for full-stack and AI/ML roles, as confirmed in the September 7, 2026 portfolio review. They need to quickly understand his focus, inspect working software, and assess his engineering decisions.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio connecting applied machine learning with usable full-stack products. Help visitors move from a clear introduction to relevant work, implementation evidence, and direct contact.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, curious. Preserve the warm paper and ink identity, light Geist display typography, and restrained three-dimensional project marks defined in the existing my-portfolio-design skill.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic agency claims, invented results, inflated seniority, decorative dashboards, dense technology inventories, and speculative projects presented as completed work. Preserve the established palette rather than introducing a new brand.

## Design Principles

1. Put the strongest inspectable work first.
2. Explain the problem before the implementation.
3. Connect skills to concrete project evidence.
4. Distinguish reported model results, working demos, and future work.
5. Keep contact and project navigation easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive links, and layouts usable from narrow mobile screens to desktop. Decorative WebGL must not be necessary to read or navigate the portfolio.
