Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026. This page covers Certior and the rest of benjamintakaki.com, run by Benjamin Takaki.

Certior has no accounts. It does not collect your name, email or anything else about you, and it counts how the app is used only as anonymous daily totals (below). Videos come from YouTube, and they only load if you choose to turn them on.

Anonymous daily totals

To see whether Certior is useful, the app sends us counts, added up per day, such as how many devices opened it, how many clips were watched from each side, and how many stories were shared. When you leave the app, it sends one small list of numbers like "visits: 1, clips watched from Center: 3". It also sends how long ago the device first opened the app (in ranges such as "2 to 7 days"), whether it was opened from the Home Screen, whether videos are on, and which site or link brought you, if any.

There is no cookie, no identifier and no IP address in what we store, so the totals can't be traced back to you or your device, and one day's totals can't be linked to another's.

YouTube

Certior uses YouTube API Services to find clips and shows them in YouTube's embedded player. By using Certior you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

If you choose Turn On Videos, YouTube serves the video content directly to your device. It can place cookies and similar technology on your device, collect information about you and what you watch, and show ads in the player. When we checked, these included the cookies VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE and YSC. If you choose Text Only, the player does not load and no images or video come from YouTube until you follow a link there.

Certior does not ask for access to your Google or YouTube account. What it gets from YouTube is public information about videos, such as titles and upload times, and it keeps that for about three days.

What is saved on your device

Certior uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember these things. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your choice about videos
  • Your settings and the last topic you opened
  • Which clips you have watched
  • Whether you have already seen the sideways swipe hint
  • How many clips you watched from each side over the last 7 days, which is shown back to you as Your mix
  • Your recent searches
  • When you last left the app, so Recap can show what's new since
  • A copy of the app and the last stories it loaded, so it opens without signal
  • The day you first opened the app and the last day it was counted, so each device is counted once a day

Change your mind

Open Settings to turn videos off, clear your watched history, or reset everything on the device. Cookies YouTube has already set stay on your device until you clear them in your browser settings, because we cannot remove them for you.

Services that handle requests

Like any website, these services see your IP address and basic request details when your device connects, and may keep them in their logs. We do not use this to identify you.

  • Render and Cloudflare host and deliver this site.
  • GitHub delivers the story feed.
  • Neon stores the anonymous daily totals.

The rest of this site

The rest of benjamintakaki.com sets no cookies and has no analytics. Its fonts are hosted here, not by a third party.
