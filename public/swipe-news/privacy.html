Privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026. This page covers Swipe News and the rest of benjamintakaki.com, run by Benjamin Takaki.

Swipe News has no accounts and no analytics. It does not collect your name, email or anything else about you. Videos come from YouTube, and they only load if you choose to turn them on.

YouTube

Swipe News uses YouTube API Services to find clips and shows them in YouTube's embedded player. By using Swipe News you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

If you choose Play videos, YouTube serves the video content directly to your device. It can place cookies and similar technology on your device, collect information about you and what you watch, and show ads in the player. When we checked, these included the cookies VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE and YSC. If you choose Text only, the player does not load and no images or video come from YouTube until you follow a link there.

Swipe News does not ask for access to your Google or YouTube account. What it gets from YouTube is public information about videos, such as titles and upload times, and it keeps that for about three days.

What is saved on your device

Swipe News uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember these things. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your choice about videos
  • Your settings and the last topic you opened
  • Which clips you have watched
  • Whether you have already seen the sideways swipe hint
  • How many clips you watched from each side over the last 7 days, which is shown back to you as Your mix
  • Your Blind Pick choices, and where you said you lean if you told it

Change your mind

Blind Pick choices can hint at your political views, so they are never sent anywhere and we can't see them. Open Settings to turn videos off, clear your watched history or your Blind Pick results, or reset everything on the device. Cookies YouTube has already set stay on your device until you clear them in your browser settings, because we cannot remove them for you.

Services that handle requests

Like any website, these services see your IP address and basic request details when your device connects, and may keep them in their logs. We do not use this to identify you.

  • Render and Cloudflare host and deliver this site.
  • GitHub delivers the story feed.

The rest of this site

The rest of benjamintakaki.com sets no cookies and has no analytics. Its fonts are hosted here, not by a third party.
